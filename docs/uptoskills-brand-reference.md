# UptoSkills public brand reference

This compact reference is based on the public BrandKit repository published by the UptoSkills GitHub organization. It supports brand and product questions; it is not an HR policy or internship workflow document.

## Positioning and audience

UptoSkills describes its purpose as connecting colleges, candidates, and corporates through an AI-powered ecosystem for hiring, engagement, and employer branding. Public audience descriptions include fresher/candidate career readiness, college employability and placement support, and corporate skills-first hiring.

The public brand guide calls for clear, approachable, professional communication. For students and freshers, use encouraging language and simple instructions. For HR partners, be confident and solution-focused. For faculty, be professional and informative.

## Public brand palette

The BrandKit lists audience colors: student blue `#355FB6`, HR orange `#F76E38`, and faculty teal `#19B19C`. It recommends adequate contrast and unmodified official logo files. These are brand reference values. Keep the separately approved Stitch assistant layout and palette unchanged unless the project owner approves a UI change.

## Limits

This source describes brand identity and public product positioning. It does not establish current internship eligibility, application status, onboarding steps, attendance rules, stipend, certificate eligibility, or HR procedures. For those questions, use a current official listing or verified policy document; otherwise say the rule could not be verified and offer human support.
