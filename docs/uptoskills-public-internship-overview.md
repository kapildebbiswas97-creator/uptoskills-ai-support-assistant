# UptoSkills public internship overview

The official public “Intern with UptoSkills” page describes work-from-home internships, commonly listed as three to six months, with work on real projects and a completion certificate. The page also describes flexible timing, mentorship, and a recognition program. These are general page descriptions; check the current role listing for its exact terms and availability.

## Finding an opening

Open the official internship page and review the live role listings. Each listing may have its own role description, requirements, duration, work arrangement, and application link. The job-specific listing is the source for current eligibility and process details. Do not infer selection chances, status, stipend, or offer decisions from the general page.

## When details are not published

If a current listing or official guide does not answer an applicant’s question, explain that the assistant cannot verify the detail from the available public guidance and offer human support. Do not invent an application workflow or claim an application was submitted.
